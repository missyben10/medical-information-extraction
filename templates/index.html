About Us & System Overview

Welcome to the Medical Information Extraction System. We are a specialized clinical decision support research group dedicated to transforming unstructured narrative clinical documentation into structured, actionable medical intelligence.

Our Core Mission

To bridge the gap between unstructured electronic health records (EHR) and structured clinical analytics, enabling rapid entity extraction, drug safety monitoring, and automated severity risk stratification.

NLP Engineering Architecture

Leveraging multi-dictionary term matching, regular expression entity extraction, temporal logic parsers, and heuristic risk classification for Diseases, Symptoms, Medications, Dosage, Duration, and Adverse Drug Reactions (ADR).
